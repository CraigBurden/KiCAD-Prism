<h2 class="prism-section-title">What your team does with it</h2>

<div class="prism-features">
	<a href="/KiCAD-Prism/use/viewing-designs/">
		<strong>View designs</strong>
		<span>Read schematics, boards and the 3D model in the browser, and cross-probe between them.</span>
	</a>
	<a href="/KiCAD-Prism/use/design-comparison/">
		<strong>Compare revisions</strong>
		<span>Review what changed between two commits, one change at a time.</span>
	</a>
	<a href="/KiCAD-Prism/use/comments/">
		<strong>Review with comments</strong>
		<span>Comment on parts, nets and areas, discuss in threads and resolve them.</span>
	</a>
	<a href="/KiCAD-Prism/use/release-studio/">
		<strong>Release a board</strong>
		<span>Build manufacturing documents, get sign-off and publish the release.</span>
	</a>
	<a href="/KiCAD-Prism/use/library-manager/">
		<strong>Share one component library</strong>
		<span>Create, import, review and release KiCad components, then place them in KiCad.</span>
	</a>
	<a href="/KiCAD-Prism/automate/overview/">
		<strong>Automate it</strong>
		<span>Drive Prism from scripts, CI, AI assistants and other services.</span>
	</a>
</div>
